Models

Run open-source models on your own computer with Ollama. One click to download, one click to use.

Skills

Teach your bots how you like things done. A skill is a SKILL.md: when to use it, then step-by-step instructions, plus any templates or scripts it needs. Bots only load a skill when a task calls for it, and skills can't skip your approvals. Only add skills from people you trust.

Shopping

Watch product pages, deal feeds and X. SlopBot verifies the real store listing, then asks you Buy / Skip / Open here and on Telegram. Your Buy covers that exact item, seller and maximum total; the shopper bot checks out with the payment saved in your store account and confirms the receipt.

Deal sources

Where deals come from and how stores are read. Keys you add stay in your system keychain on this computer and are only shown masked.

Vault

Logins and API keys your bots can use but never see. Encrypted on this Mac with a key kept in your Keychain, so the file is useless anywhere else. Each one only works on the websites you list, and using it asks you first.

Channels

Talk to your bots from your phone while SlopBot runs on this computer. Nothing is exposed to the internet: SlopBot checks Telegram for your messages.

Connectors

Give your bots new tools from MCP servers. Read-only tools run freely; anything else asks you first.
